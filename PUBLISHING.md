# Before Publishing: Open Questions

The brief says to confirm API access and what xAI's terms let us say publicly *before* the recording goes out. This page tracks those questions. It is a checklist, not legal advice; someone who owns the press campaign has to sign off on it.

## 1. API access

- [ ] The MeetStream account used for the demo can create an agent with `model.provider: "xai"` (`npm run check`, then one real run).
- [ ] Confirm with MeetStream whose xAI usage this is: MeetStream's own xAI account behind MIA, or a key we attach. That decides which xAI terms apply to us directly.

## 2. What xAI's published guidelines say

From the [xAI Brand Guidelines](https://x.ai/legal/brand-guidelines). Re-read the live page before relying on this; it may have changed.

- xAI claims the "xAI" and "Grok" names and logos as its marks. Use them only to refer accurately to xAI or its services.
- No use that suggests xAI endorses, approves, or sponsors us. Nothing like "official Grok integration" or "partnered with xAI" unless xAI agrees to it in writing.
- The marks must not appear in an app title, domain name, or the name of a product or service xAI did not make. That is why this repo is named as a MeetStream demo and states that it is not made by xAI.
- **For press releases, the guidelines ask you to contact `legal@x.ai` before mentioning xAI in press materials.** This is the main blocker for the press campaign.
- They ask for an attribution such as "Created with Grok" on content generated with Grok.

The API terms are xAI's [Enterprise Terms of Service](https://x.ai/legal/terms-of-service-enterprise). Check them for clauses on publicity, naming customers, and demonstrating the service.

## 3. Wording

Safer, because it is factual:

- "Grok, running through MeetStream, joins the call and answers out loud."
- "Built with MeetStream MIA, using xAI's Grok as the voice model."
- "Created with Grok" on the clip.

Clear these before using them:

- Anything with "partner", "official", "powered by xAI", or "integration with xAI".
- The Grok or xAI logo in the video, thumbnail, or lockup. The existing ad ends on a "Grok × meetstream.ai" lockup, so that needs a decision too.
- Naming the bot `Grok` in the call is an accurate description, since it is Grok answering. Still, mention it when you write to xAI.
